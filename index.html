The workflow

From original artwork
to a working storefront

Two print concepts became photographic garment concepts. Exact source graphics were composited onto generated cotton bases, then front and strict-profile anchors guided the video turns.

Selected footage was keyed, registered at its wooden apex and packed into transparent frame atlases.

Hover starts a short turn. Leaving reverses it from its current frame. The wire stays fixed, while neighboring garments slide aside at full opacity. On touch screens, swipe the rail and tap to inspect.
